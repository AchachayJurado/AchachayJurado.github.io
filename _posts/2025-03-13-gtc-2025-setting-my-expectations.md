As I get ready to attend NVIDIA's GPU Technology Conference (**GTC 2025**) in San Jose from March 17–21, I keep thinking about the earlier industry conferences — HANNOVER MESSE, Embedded World — that shaped my path in tech. GTC feels like the next one of those defining moments.

### Before the conference

On Sunday I'm attending a full-day workshop on **Building AI Agents with MultiModal Models**. I want to understand how multimodal systems communicate and interact with one another to build solutions that are reliable and deterministic.

### What I'm looking forward to

- **Dell / Latent AI booth (1423):** we'll be demoing an optimized edge AI pose-estimation model running locally on hardware — with a chance to win a Dell Precision laptop.
- **Jensen Huang's keynote:** I'm expecting announcements around **Physical AI** and how it gets integrated.
- **Technical sessions:** MatX numerical computing, real-time edge computing, and healthcare AI innovations.
- **Braindate:** I'll be leading a discussion on **Hardware-aware AI development**, focusing on data-driven versus model-based approaches.

### On a personal note

I recently relocated to the USA, and this trip carries some emotional weight — it's the first time I'll be away from my toddler. It's a reminder that this journey is more of a marathon than a sprint.

GTC 2025 will be a defining moment for tracking where AI is heading, especially within the edge computing space. I'm excited to see it unfold.
